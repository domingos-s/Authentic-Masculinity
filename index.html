How it works

Set up once, then add each reading as the group progresses.

  1. 01

    Choose your AI

    Use ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, or another capable LLM.

  2. 02

    Copy the study instructions

    Paste PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.txt into the AI's project, workspace, or custom-instruction area when available.

  3. 03

    Add the chapter files

    Upload both the chapter PDF and its companion knowledge file.

  4. 04

    Start studying

    Ask questions, prepare for group discussion, examine biblical arguments, or use the AI for private personal reflection.
